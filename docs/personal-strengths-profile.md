## Approved concise application version

I work well with different kinds of people and am good at keeping communication clear and disagreements constructive. I also use AI effectively because I can define problems and explain what I need precisely. At work, I care more about finding the right answer than proving myself right, and I’m happy to change my mind when someone makes a convincing case. I’m also curious and creative, with a genuine interest in classical music, literature and art.

## Writing rules

- Describe what Andy does through verbs and behaviour rather than listing abstract skills or fields of knowledge.
- Do not say that Andy "knows philosophy" or has a "philosophical background" unless the question specifically asks about education or intellectual interests. Express the practical result instead: he examines assumptions, understands different viewpoints, communicates clearly and handles disagreement constructively.
- Do not use `organising people`. When relevant, describe him as working well with different personalities, understanding what people need, coordinating collaboration or keeping communication constructive.
- Prefer natural spoken English over formal competency language.
- Avoid directly claiming `I am smart`, `I am interesting` or `I am humorous`. Show these qualities through judgement, curiosity, interests and behaviour.
- Choose only the two or three traits most relevant to the exact question and word limit; do not compress every trait into every answer.
- All examples and claims must remain grounded in Andy's verified experience.

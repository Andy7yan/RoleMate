# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in OpenJobAgent free from harassment, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technical preference.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should be respectful, accept technical feedback, keep discussion focused on the work, and protect any personal information encountered during support. Harassment, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disclosure of private information and sustained disruption are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and communication that breach this code. Reports should use a private repository contact or security channel. Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy where possible and apply a response proportionate to the conduct.

This text is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
